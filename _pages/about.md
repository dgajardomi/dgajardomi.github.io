I am currently a temporary teaching assistant and researcher (Attaché Temporaire d’Enseignement et de Recherche, ATER) at the Institut de Mathématiques de Toulouse.

I obtained my PhD in Mathematics at the Institut de Mathématiques de Toulouse under the supervision of Prof.
<a href="https://www.math.univ-toulouse.fr/~fboyer/" target="_blank">Franck Boyer</a> and Prof. Michel Fournié. Previously, I completed my Master’s degree in Mathematics under the supervision of Prof. <a href="https://amercado.mat.utfsm.cl/" target="_blank">Alberto Mercado</a> at the Universidad Técnica Federico Santa María in Chile.

My research interests lie primarily in the analysis of partial differential equations. I am particularly interested in the study of fluid–structure interaction problems, with a focus on questions related to well-posedness, stabilization, and numerical aspects. I am also interested in inverse problems.
