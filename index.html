I obtained my PhD in Mathematics at the Institut de Mathématiques de Toulouse in France under the supervision of Prof. Franck Boyer and Prof. Michel Fournié. Previously, I completed my Master’s degree in Mathematics under the supervision of Prof. Alberto Mercado at the Universidad Técnica Federico Santa María in Chile.

My research interests lie primarily in the analysis of partial differential equations. I am interested in questions involving the well-posedness, control, inverse problems and numerical aspects. I am particularly interested in the study of fluid–structure interaction systems.
