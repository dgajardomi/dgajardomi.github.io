In November 2025, I completed my PhD in Mathematics at the Institut de Mathématiques de Toulouse in France, under the supervision of Professors Franck Boyer and Michel Fournié. Previously, I obtained my Master’s degree in Mathematics at the Universidad Técnica Federico Santa María in Chile, under the supervision of Professor Alberto Mercado.

My research interests lie primarily in the analysis of partial differential equations. I am interested in questions involving the well-posedness, control, inverse problems and numerical aspects. I am particularly interested in the study of fluid–structure interaction systems.
